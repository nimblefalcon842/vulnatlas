Disclosure policy

We coordinate every disclosure. The process: a finding is verified, reported privately to the vendor, and a fix is coordinated. Publication follows the agreed disclosure timeline. Our default coordination window is 90 days; where a vendor agreement states otherwise, the vendor agreement governs. Public reports contain sanitized proof-of-concept steps only, never working exploit code.

We credit researchers for their findings, unless they prefer to remain anonymous. Our default is private coordinated disclosure: we notify the affected vendor privately before publishing anything that identifies them. Publication without prior private notice happens only where private notification is not possible, or where the affected project's own published disclosure rules direct otherwise.

To report a vulnerability you believe affects our published work, write to <a href="mailto:vulnatlas@proton.me">vulnatlas@proton.me</a>.
