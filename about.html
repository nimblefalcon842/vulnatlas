About VulnAtlas Research

VulnAtlas Research is a vulnerability research lab. We find real bugs in real software, coordinate responsible disclosure with vendors, and publish our findings as CVE reports and in-depth security writeups.

Our work is open: every advisory follows responsible disclosure timelines, credits the researchers who found it, and explains the bug well enough that defenders can act on it. We publish research because security improves when it is shared.

The lab runs a simple discipline: public source and static analysis first, locally built instances for verification, and a documented audit trail for every finding.
